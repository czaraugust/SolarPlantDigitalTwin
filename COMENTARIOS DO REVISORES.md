Reviewer #3 Comments

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

1\. The title mentions the "effects on inverter mission profile, lifetime, and reliability." However, the current results do not include any data on these three aspects. Even though the authors state this data will be included in the full paper, the digest must still present the main innovative results of the study.

2\. Regarding the tilt angles, the authors only selected 5°, 10°, and 15°. Could you please explain why only these three angles were chosen? Testing only these angles makes the results seem a bit limited.

3\. The authors used only 16 days of data to evaluate the results. I believe this is not enough for estimating the lifetime. The sun's position changes with the four seasons throughout the year, which will significantly change the collected data. Because of this, the optimal angle will likely change as well. Using a full year of data would be much more convincing.

4\. The figures in the paper are too small, making it difficult to read the data. Please improve the size and quality of the figures.

Reviewer #4 Comments

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

This paper proposes a comprehensive framework to analyze the impact of PV array positioning and DC oversizing on inverter mission profiles, lifetime, and reliability. The proposed multi-phase methodology systematically links solar geometry, electrical behavior, thermal stress, and reliability metrics, which is well structured and meaningful. However, several aspects could be improved:

1\. While a seven-phase framework is proposed, the presented results are limited to the early stages (up to Phase 4). The key outcomes related to lifetime estimation and reliability (Phases 6–7) are not yet demonstrated.

2\. The experimental analysis is based on a relatively short 16-day dataset, which may not be sufficient to fully support conclusions regarding long-term reliability.

3\. The framework mainly integrates established modeling and lifetime estimation techniques, with limited new analytical or modeling contributions.

4\. The work focuses primarily on system-level and reliability aspects, with limited discussion of converter-level design or control implications.
